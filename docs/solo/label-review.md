# Label review (E2) — for the operator

The 100 eval lessons were written and labelled by Opus 5.5. A second pass (Sonnet 5.5) labelled every teacher line
again **without seeing** the first label. They agree on **735 of 800** lines (91.9%).

**What to do (about 20–30 minutes):**
1. **Disagreements** — for each one, write after `Pick:` either `A` (first label), `B` (second label) or the
   right tag yourself. Tags: ask_to_explain, compare_methods, concrete_materials, targeted_question, think_aloud, new_sum, check_whole_class, invite_quiet_child, specific_praise, tell_answer, repeat_same_way, move_on_or_scold, other, off_task.
2. **Spot checks** — 40 random lines where both agreed. Leave `OK: yes` if the label is right; otherwise write
   `OK: no <right tag>`. The share of "yes" is reported as the answer key's quality.
3. Save the file and tell me "labels done". I apply your picks with `evals/label-check.ts --apply`.

Short guide: ask_to_explain = asks a child HOW they got it · compare_methods = asks two or more to compare/each explain · concrete_materials = uses bundles/sticks/objects/drawings · targeted_question = a precise question on the exact wrong step (the borrowed ten, opening a bundle, the ones column) · think_aloud = solves it herself step by step aloud · new_sum = gives a whole new sum (or asks the current one again) with no other teaching · check_whole_class = thumbs up / who agrees · invite_quiet_child = gently invites a child who hasn't spoken · specific_praise = praises a specific thing · tell_answer = gives the right answer / tells them to repeat it · repeat_same_way = repeats the same explanation, louder · move_on_or_scold = dismisses, scolds, "wrong, next" · other = greetings, classroom management, plain "good" · off_task = not about the lesson.
