Hello 🙏 We are three students from SRMIST building a practice tool for teachers: **Shikshakalay**. In 5 minutes you
teach 2-digit subtraction to four pretend children (Ravi, Arjun, Meena, Sana) who make real children's mistakes. At
the end you get a private coaching card that quotes your own words.

🔗 https://shikshakalay.onrender.com (the first load can take 30 seconds)
🆔 ID: ____   🔑 PIN: ____

1. Log in with your ID and PIN, pick **English** or **हिन्दी** at the top, tap "I agree".
2. Tap **Start practice**. Hold the round mic button, speak to the children, then let go. (You can also type.)
3. Things to try:
   • Ask "What is 52 minus 27?" — listen to the different answers.
   • Ask one child "How did you get that?" — then help that child with bundles and sticks.
   • Invite shy Sana by name.
4. Tap **End lesson** and read your card. Under it, answer **2 quick questions** (30 seconds).

One lesson today and one tomorrow would help us a lot. Your voice recording is not kept, and only you can see your
card. Any questions — just reply here. Thank you!
