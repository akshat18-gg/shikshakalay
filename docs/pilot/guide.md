# Shikshakalay — pilot guide (1 page)

**What it is:** a 5-minute practice lesson. You teach 2-digit subtraction to four pretend children (Ravi, Arjun,
Meena, Sana). They make real mistakes — the same ones children make in class. Speak in Hindi or English. At the end
you get a private coaching card that quotes your own words and gives one line to try in class tomorrow.

**Start (phone or laptop):**
1. Open **https://shikshakalay.onrender.com** (the first load can take ~30 seconds).
2. Log in with the ID and PIN we sent you. Choose **हिन्दी** or **English** at the top.
3. Read the short consent note and tap **I agree**.
4. Tap **Start practice**. Hold the round mic button, speak to the class, release. (You can also type.)
5. When you are done (or after 5 minutes), tap **End lesson** and read your card. Tap ▶ to hear the line for tomorrow.

**What to try:**
- Ask a sum ("बावन में से सत्ताईस कितना होगा?") and listen to the different answers.
- Tell them the answer — then give a new sum. Do the mistakes come back?
- Ask one child *how* they got their answer. Then try bundles and sticks with that child.
- Invite shy Sana by name.

**Please do 1 lesson today and 1 tomorrow**, then tap "Did you try the line?" on the home page. Two short feedback
questions take 30 seconds.
